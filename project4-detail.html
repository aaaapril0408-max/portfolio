Project Detail

Cozyla Calendar+ & Mobile App

A family scheduling experience designed to help parents and children manage routines, tasks, and shared moments with simplicity and delight.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Timeline

Jan 2026 — Apr 2026

Contribution

Research, interaction design, visual system, prototype.

This project explores a calm, family-friendly interface for shared schedules, quick task handoff, and household routines. It prioritizes clarity for both adults and children through color-coded workflows, simple task cards, and a visually warm layout.

The mobile app concept includes a daily agenda, rewards system, and easy access to family tasks. It is designed to feel familiar yet refreshing for families managing multiple activities together.
